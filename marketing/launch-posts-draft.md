## Post 3 — Indie Hackers ("Show IH")

**Format:** title + body post in the [Show IH](https://www.indiehackers.com/group/show-ih)
group. Long-form, technical, building-in-public tone. IH audience
loves stack details, business model decisions, and honest reflection.

**Title:**

> Show IH: Kultivar — privacy-first grow journal for South African
> growers (Android-first launch, Flutter)

**Body:**

> Hey IH,
>
> Pre-launch peek at what I've been building.
>
> **The product:** Kultivar is a private, offline-first cannabis
> grow tracker. Available in 9 languages including Afrikaans and
> isiZulu. Three-tier pricing (Free / one-time IAP / monthly sub).
> Android first; iOS Q2.
>
> **Live site:** https://kultivar.io
> **Source (reference):** https://github.com/KultivarSA/kultivar
>
> ---
>
> **Why I'm building it**
>
> Existing grow apps are cloud-first and ad-supported. For a hobby
> that's still legally sensitive in most places, "create an account
> to track your plants" is the wrong default. I wanted something
> that worked the way a paper journal works: yours, local, no one
> else's business.
>
> I'm also South African and the SA market just had a major legal
> shift (Cannabis for Private Purposes Act 7 of 2024). Zero quality
> apps in this space ship in Afrikaans or isiZulu. Felt like an
> obvious gap to fill before international competitors catch on.
>
> ---
>
> **Stack + interesting decisions**
>
> * **Flutter** for cross-platform from day one (Android first, iOS
>   shares the codebase)
> * **Hive** for local persistence + **SharedPreferences** for
>   simple settings. No sqlite — overkill for the data shape
> * **Supabase** for the optional Pro Cloud backend (community
>   benchmarks + planned E2EE sync). Stays a no-op until the user
>   explicitly opts in
> * **RevenueCat** for cross-store subscription handling
> * **GitHub Pages** for the landing page + privacy/terms hosting
>   (hosted with Cloudflare in front for CDN + edge analytics)
> * **CIPC (SA company registry)** — registered "Kultivar (Pty) Ltd"
>   the same week I pushed the first commit. R175 + 30 minutes
>
> Pricing model — three tiers because two felt like a forced binary:
>
> * **Free:** 1 grow space, 3 active plants, 60-day analytics.
>   Enough to complete a first cycle without hitting a paywall mid-
>   grow.
> * **Lifetime Local (R299 one-time):** removes every cap, no
>   recurring fee. The "I just want to track my plants forever"
>   tier.
> * **Pro Cloud (R59/month):** optional community percentile
>   benchmarks now; E2EE cross-device sync in v1.1.
>
> Free isn't a trial. The cap design is "complete a first cycle
> end-to-end" — by the time you outgrow it, you have data you don't
> want to lose, which is the natural upgrade trigger.
>
> ---
>
> **What I've shipped pre-launch**
>
> * 305 tests passing, `flutter analyze` clean
> * Pre-flight script (`tools/preflight.ps1`) running 6 release gates
> * Release pipeline (`tools/release.ps1`) — one-command AAB build +
>   git tag
> * Privacy Policy + Terms hosted at branded URLs
> * Apple + Google review notes pre-drafted
> * Launch plan (`LAUNCH.md`) + Android execution checklist
>   (`LAUNCH_ANDROID.md`)
> * v1.1 roadmap (`ROADMAP.md`) with E2EE sync spec
>
> All of those are in the public repo — partly because I want to
> show what shipping-state looks like for an indie launch, partly
> because the privacy-first brand demands transparency.
>
> ---
>
> **What I'm asking**
>
> 1. **Eyes on the landing page.** Does the value prop land in 5
>    seconds? https://kultivar.io
> 2. **Pricing feedback.** Three tiers, intentional cap on Free.
>    Reasonable? Brutal?
> 3. **Anything I'm missing pre-launch?** I'm in the "waiting on Play
>    Console verification" window so anything I can prep now is
>    welcome
> 4. **SA / 🇿🇦 founders specifically** — any advice on dealing with
>    SARS for SaaS revenue or Play Console payouts to TymeBank?
>
> Happy to answer any tech questions about the stack, sync
> architecture, or the launch playbook.
>
> Cheers,
> <<your name>>
