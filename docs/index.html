Everything a serious grower needs

Track each cycle from seed to cure, then keep a permanent record you can share or revisit.

Multi-space grow management

Organise plants across veg tents, flower rooms, dry rooms, and clone stations. Each space carries its own targets and reminders.

Photo timeline + flipbook

Capture growth weekly. Auto-organises into a swipeable timeline and an animated flipbook so progress is impossible to miss.

Cost-per-gram tracking

Log expenses by category (seeds, nutrients, IPM, substrate, electricity, water). Get an exact cost-per-gram on every completed run.

Environment monitoring

Log temperature, humidity, and VPD per space. Charts highlight drift outside your target window so you act before it matters.

Built-in strain library

~150 strains with flowering time, terpene profile, expected yield, and training recommendations. Add your own or extend an existing entry.

Shareable Grow Reports

Every completed cycle becomes a one-page PDF you can keep as a personal archive or share with your grower community.

Cross-grow analytics

Compare runs side by side. Yield-per-strain, stage-duration drift, environment overlays — find out what actually moved the needle.

Voice notes & tags

Capture what your hands can’t type. Free-form tags surface across notes, photos, and reminders.

Stage-aware reminders

Watering and feeding cadences shift automatically as plants move from veg to flower to dry to cure.
