2. Your data, your responsibility

The bulk of your grow records, photos, notes, and harvest logs are stored locally on your device. We do not have a copy.

reset and you don't have a backup, we cannot recover your grow history. There is nowhere on our infrastructure where it exists.

decrypted without it, and we cannot reset it.
