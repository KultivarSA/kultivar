Siyakwemukela ku-Kultivar 1.0 — idayari yokukhulisa ebeka ubumfihlo kuqala, kusukela embewini kuya ekuvuthweni.

Idayari ngesitshalo ngasinye, i-VPD, ukuhlola isivuno, izindleko ngegremu, imibiko ye-PDF nelabhulali yezinhlobo.

Iyatholakala ngezilimi eziyi-9, kuhlanganise nesiBhunu nesiZulu.

Izinga lamahhala lihlanganisa ukukhulisa kokuqala. I-Lifetime Local ivula konke ngokuthenga kanye; i-Pro Cloud yengeza izilinganiso zomphakathi (kuza maduze).

Idayari yakho ihlala ikhiyiwe kudivayisi.
