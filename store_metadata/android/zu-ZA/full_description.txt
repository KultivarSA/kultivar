I-Kultivar yidayari yokukhulisa ekhelwe abakhuliseli basekhaya abafuna ukukhumbula okuye kwasebenza — nokungasebenzanga.

Landela isitshalo ngasinye kusukela embewini kuya ekuvuthweni. Bhala ukondla, ukuthena, ukuchelela, ukulawula izinambuzane (i-IPM), nemibono — konke kuxhumene nelabhulali yezinhlobo. Bona umkhiqizo wakho ophakathi nendawo, izindleko ngegremu, nombiko opheleleyo we-PDF ngemva kweseshini ngayinye yokukhulisa. Idatha yakho ihlala kuthebhuleti yakho ngokuzenzakalelayo.

UMTHETHO ENINGIZIMU AFRIKA

UMthetho weCannabis ngezinhloso eziyimfihlo (uMthetho 7 wa-2024) wenza ukukhulisa kwabantu abadala ngokwabo kube semthethweni ekusebenziseni komuntu siqu. I-Kultivar ngumlandi womuntu siqu — akukho ukudayisa, akukho ukuletha, akuyona imakethe. Hlala usebenzelana ngokuthembekile nemithetho yedolobhakazi yakho yendawo.

KUNGANI ABAKHULISI BASEBENZISA I-KULTIVAR

• Idayari yesitshalo eshibalalayo. Thola ngokuthepha okuthathu: "yiziphi izinto zokondla engazisebenzisa ku-Blue Dream ehlobo elidlule?"
• Umshini wezifundo ophelele — Kuyakhula → Kuvuniwe → Kuyoma → Kuyavuthwa → Kuqedwe.
• Ukurekhoda i-environment ngendawo (itende / igumbi / ngaphandle) ngezinga lokushisa, umswakama, isibali se-VPD, nezexwayiso ngaphandle kwebanga.
• Izikhumbuzo ezivumelana nesigaba — ukuchelela nokondla kuyazilungiselela phakathi kwesithombo, i-veg, i-bloom, ne-flush.
• Umugqa wesikhathi wezithombe ngesitshalo ngasinye nge-overlay yedethi nomngcele oqhakambisa isigaba.
• Ukuhlolwa kwekhwalithi ngesikhathi sokuvuna — izinkanyezi ezingu-½ zenani lonke + iphunga + umphumela + ukubukeka.
• Isilandeli sezindleko — bhala izindleko ngamamali angu-22 (kuhlanganise i-Rand R), bona izindleko ngegremu.
• Ilabhulali yezinhlobo enokuqhathaniswa — ihlanganisa izinsuku zembali, futhi yengeza ezakho.
• Ukuqhathanisa kokukhulisa okwehlukene.
• Imibiko yokukhulisa — ileli yokukhulisa ngayinye iqedwe ikhiqiza i-PDF.
• Amanothi ezwi.
• Amalebula okuzikhethela ezitshalweni nasemanothini.
• Isipele + ukubuyisa nge-AES-256-GCM enekhrisithwe.
• Ukuvalwa kohlelo nge-biometrics + i-PIN.
• Izilimi eziyi-9 ezizenzelayo — IsiNgisi, IsiJalimane, IsiSpanish, IsiFulentshi, Isi-Italian, IsiDashi, IsiPutukezi, IsiBhunu, IsiZulu.
