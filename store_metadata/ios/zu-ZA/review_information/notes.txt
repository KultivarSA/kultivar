KUYINI I-KULTIVAR

Idayari eyimfihlo, esekelwe endaweni yangaphandle, yabakhuliseli abadala basekhaya ezindaweni lapho ukukhulisa i-cannabis kungokomthetho. Kuyilandelelo kuphela: ayikho imakethe, akukho ukuhweba phakathi kwabasebenzisi, akukho ukulethwa, akukho ukuthengwa kwe-cannabis noma imikhiqizo ye-cannabis.

ISISEKELO SOMTHETHO ENINGIZIMU AFRIKA

UMthetho weCannabis ngezinhloso eziyimfihlo (uMthetho 7 wa-2024) wenza ukukhulisa kwabantu abadala ngokwabo, kube semthethweni. I-Kultivar iyidayari yomuntu siqu yalaba basebenzisi.
